## Logo

Use case: logo-brand. Create the final square hackathon submission logo for ZeroKey Mate, based on the actual app face in the reference screenshot. Transform this screenshot into a clean standalone square logo: preserve the defining character of the face, two friendly tall white oval eyes with simple charcoal outlines and small dark oval pupils, gently attentive, just touching, no mouth and no other facial features. Remove the phone status bar and all screenshot UI. Center the eyes as a strong iconic mark taking about 70 percent of the square width. Solid warm ivory background #f7f6ee, very dark forest-charcoal outlines #213b2c. Crisp flat graphic, balanced and welcoming, immediately legible at small size. A premium playful on-device companion, not a technical security emblem. No words, letters, locks, keyholes, badges, circuitry, robots, hands, watermark, frame, drop shadow, or extra decorations. Output one final square image, 1024 by 1024 pixels if possible. Reference image is only for the existing Mate eye design.

An elegant editorial product illustration, clearly a designed cover rather than a screenshot or evidence photo. Warm ivory paper backdrop #f7f6ee, deep forest green #213b2c type, restrained natural soft shadows. Left half: exceptionally clear beautiful large typography reading exactly "ZeroKey Mate"; underneath medium size "Your companion. No other text, no vendor logos, no padlocks, no token logos, no fake charts, no QR codes, no identity cards, no human faces, no extra screens or UI, no neon cyberpunk aesthetic. One cohesive image.
