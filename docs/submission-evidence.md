The [official guide](https://ethglobal.com/events/ethonline2026/info/details), rechecked September 13, sets the deadline at **September 14, 2026, 01:00 JST** (September 13, 12:00 EDT). It requires 2–4 minutes, at least 720p and human narration; it disallows sped-up footage, AI/TTS voiceover and mobile-phone filming of the submission. The supplied stand segment was filmed with another phone; no exception has been confirmed. Edited takes are disclosed as such. The evidence figure of 12.0 s is one app-level local proof-processing observation, not a general ProveKit benchmark.

Do not publish card passwords, raw identity data, account credentials or private deployment files. Only a submitted state verified after reload closes the submission task.
