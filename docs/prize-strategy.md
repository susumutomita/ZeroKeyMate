Official requirements: [Arc](https://ethglobal.com/events/ethonline2026/prizes/arc), [Privy](https://ethglobal.com/events/ethonline2026/prizes/privy), [The Graph](https://ethglobal.com/events/ethonline2026/prizes/the-graph).

The Arc Agentic Economy category requires meaningful Circle Agent Stack usage, which is not present in the demonstrated checkout. The current target is the DeFi/Onchain Finance payment category. A historic local specialist/vault adapter is not evidence of Circle Agent Stack use in this purchase.

ProveKit from World Foundation is central to the local age proof, but World ID authentication, AgentKit and Selfie Check are not integrated. ProveKit alone does not establish eligibility for the [World partner tracks](https://ethglobal.com/events/ethonline2026/prizes/world). ENS and other sponsors are not current targets. Do not add an unrelated SDK merely to fill the third partner slot.

The [official guide](https://ethglobal.com/events/ethonline2026/info/details) allows up to three partner selections and requires accurate AI/reused-work disclosure. The entrant's dashboard shows the from-scratch project rules; confirm the applicable contribution history before final submission. Prepared copy and working transactions do not mean the entry has been submitted.
